About GoSnel

Discover the story behind GoSnel (pronounced "Go-Snel", spelled G-O-S-N-E-L with a single L) - the UAE's most innovative food discovery platform powered by artificial intelligence.

Our Story

GoSnel was born from a simple observation: people in the UAE spend too much time deciding what to eat and where to order from. With thousands of restaurants across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and other emirates, choice paralysis is real.

Our name, GoSnel (with a single 'L'), represents our mission: "Go Snell" - Dutch for "Go Fast". We believe food discovery should be instant, personalized, and delightful.

Unlike generic food delivery apps, GoSnel uses advanced AI to learn your preferences, dietary restrictions, and mood to suggest the perfect meal every time.
