About GoSnel

Cheap, fresh and local. Fast to Your Door. GoSnel brings everyday services to residents in towers and buildings across the UAE.

Our story

Building life should feel simple. Residents need grocery, pharmacy, laundry, water, meals, and other local help without juggling five apps or long waits.

GoSnel connects each building with trusted shops nearby. You open one app, send a clear request, and local partners take it from there to your door.

We are building for communities first: towers, compounds, and the neighbourhood shops that already serve them.

What we stand for

  • Local by design

  • Simple for residents

  • Built for the UAE

Mission
