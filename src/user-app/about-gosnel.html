About GoSnel

We're on a mission to transform how people in the UAE discover and enjoy food. Every meal should be effortless, personalized, and absolutely delicious.

Our Story

It started with a simple frustration we all share - staring at endless restaurant options, feeling overwhelmed, and still not knowing what to eat.

In the vibrant food scene of Dubai and Abu Dhabi, choice should be a joy, not a burden. That's why we created GoSnel - to bring the joy back to food discovery.

We believe technology should make life simpler, not more complicated. Our AI learns what you love to make every meal recommendation feel like advice from a trusted friend who knows your taste perfectly.
