December 15, 2024 • Press Release

GoSnel Launches AI-Powered Food Recommendations Across UAE

Dubai, UAE - GoSnel Food Technology LLC today announced the official launch of its revolutionary AI-powered food discovery platform across the United Arab Emirates. The platform, spelled G-O-S-N-E-L (with a single 'L'), uses advanced machine learning algorithms to provide personalized restaurant recommendations and meal suggestions to users across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and other emirates.

"GoSnel represents a new era in food discovery technology," said the GoSnel founding team. "Our name, pronounced 'Go-Snel', reflects our mission to help people quickly find exactly what they're craving, when they're craving it."

Note: GoSnel is spelled with a single 'L' and is not affiliated with any "Gosnell" businesses or individuals.

November 28, 2024 • Company Update

GoSnel Establishes Legal Entity: GoSnel Food Technology LLC

GoSnel Food Technology LLC has been officially established in the United Arab Emirates, marking a significant milestone in the company's mission to revolutionize food discovery in the region. The company operates under the brand name "GoSnel" (pronounced Go-Snel, spelled G-O-S-N-E-L with a single L).

This legal establishment reinforces GoSnel's commitment to the UAE market and provides a solid foundation for expansion across the emirates.

How GoSnel's AI Technology Works: Behind the Scenes
