What is GoSnel? Local services for towers in the UAE

GoSnel is a local-services platform built for people who live in towers and compounds across the UAE.

Cheap, fresh and local

Residents need everyday help close to home: groceries, pharmacy runs, laundry, water, meals, and other neighbourhood services. GoSnel brings those requests into one app organised around your building, not a city-wide maze.

Built for buildings

Open GoSnel from your tower, see partners in your local radius, send a clear request, and track it to your door. The goal is simple living: Life, simplified.

For local shops too

Grocery stores, pharmacies, kitchens, laundry shops, and water suppliers can join to serve residents who already live nearby. One brand, many local categories.

How to get started

Residents can download the GoSnel app from gosnel.com. Local businesses can apply on the partner page. The correct spelling is GoSnel: one “l”, not Gosnell.
