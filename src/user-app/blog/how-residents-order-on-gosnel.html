How residents order on GoSnel: from request to doorstep

Ordering on GoSnel is designed to stay simple: building, request, doorstep.

Step 1: Start from your building

Download the app from user.gosnel.com/install and open services available in your local radius.

Step 2: Choose what you need

Pick a category such as grocery, pharmacy, laundry, water, or meals. Browse nearby partners and send a clear request.

Step 3: Receive it at home

Follow status updates as your local partner prepares and delivers to your door.

Need help?

Visit gosnel.com/how-it-works for the full overview, or gosnel.com/faq for common questions.
