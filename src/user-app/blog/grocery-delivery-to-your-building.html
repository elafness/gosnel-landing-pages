Grocery delivery to your building with GoSnel

Need milk, bread, or household staples without leaving your building? GoSnel connects you with nearby grocery partners.

Start from your building

Open the GoSnel app, pick grocery, and browse partners that serve your local radius.

Send a clear request

List what you need, confirm details, and let the shop prepare your order. Accurate lists help partners pack faster.

Receive at home

Track packing and on-the-way updates, then collect at your door or concierge as your building allows.

Why local grocery matters

Nearby minimarts already know tower routes. GoSnel gives them a structured channel to residents who live next door.
