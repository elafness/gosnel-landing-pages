GoSnel vs Gosnell: the correct spelling of our brand

If Google asks “Did you mean Gosnell?”, you are looking for GoSnel: the UAE local-services app for towers.

The correct spelling

Our brand is GoSnel (G-o-S-n-e-l). It uses a single “l”. Gosnell with a double “ll” is a different surname and search result, not our company.

Official places to find us

Visit https://gosnel.com for residents, https://gosnel.com/partner for local shops, and download the app via https://user.gosnel.com/install. Social and store listings use the same GoSnel spelling.

Why search engines confuse the names

Google often suggests more common spellings. As more people search for GoSnel, link to gosnel.com, and read our pages, the brand match gets stronger. Clear spelling on blogs, titles, and profiles helps.

Quick tip

When you share GoSnel, write the URL gosnel.com or say “GoSnel with one L.” That reduces mix-ups with Gosnell.
