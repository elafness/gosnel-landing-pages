What is Agency Partner?

GoSnel Agency Partner is built for recruitment agencies that want to place commission-based sales and recruiting talent into our partner network — not for individuals applying themselves.

You get an Agency HQ login with three invite links, partner approval tools, and residual tracking. There is no public self-apply form for this track. We onboard agencies by invitation after you contact us.

Who you recruit

SP

Restaurant Sales Partner

Visits restaurants, registers them on GoSnel, earns field commissions.

HSP

Hotel Sales Partner

Sells Revenue Booster to hotels and links nearby restaurants & B2B.

RP

Recruiter Partner

Recruits more SP and HSP through their network — fully remote.

Each agency account includes three invite links (one per track). Share them with candidates you hire or place.

How you earn

Agency Partner is commission-based. You earn a fixed 10% residual of GoSnel profit after SP/HSP (and after RP if a Recruiter Partner is in the chain), for 12 months per restaurant or hotel.

  • SP/HSP take their share of GoSnel fees first
  • If an RP recruited that partner, RP residual comes next
  • Your 10% is of the remaining GoSnel net — flat rate, no tiers

A good fit if you

  • Run a recruitment or staffing agency (or similar talent business)
  • Can source field sales and recruiter talent who want commission roles
  • Want residual income tied to partners you place — not a fixed agency retainer
