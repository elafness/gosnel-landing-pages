About the role

Complete the work and get paid

You work from a public task board in Partner HQ. Most work comes as a Building Activation Package — a clear list of businesses in one area or building that need to be registered so the location is fully served (grocery, pharmacy, restaurant, gift shop, etc.).

You can also take individual single tasks when available.

Every package or task shows the total payout before you take it. Finish the visits, upload proof, and when HQ approves — that amount is credited to your Field Project Manager wallet. Then request payout anytime (no minimum).

This is a part-time Field Project Manager role. It is the starting point of the GoSnel Partner Journey. Strong performers can later be upgraded by Admin to Local Sales Partner, Restaurant Sales Partner, Hotel Sales Partner, and beyond.

How it works

Three simple steps.

What you will do

  • Browse open Building Activation Packages and single tasks.
  • Take one package or task at a time (only one hold allowed).
  • Visit the listed businesses, present GoSnel, and collect decision-maker details + proof.
  • Upload everything in Partner HQ and mark the work complete.
  • After HQ approval, request a payout from your Field Project Manager wallet (no minimum).
  • Track progress in your Pipeline: Available → In progress → Under review → Completed.

What you get

Clear payout before you start

Every package and task shows the amount. Finish the work, get approval, and the money lands in your wallet.

Part-time with clear deadlines

48 hours to complete, plus one optional +24h extension. Fair for you and fair for other Field Project Managers.

Partner HQ tools

Tasks, My Task, Pipeline, and Payouts in one place on admin.gosnel.com.

Growth path

This is the entry point. Consistent strong performance can lead to upgrade into commission-based roles (Local → Restaurant → Hotel → Recruiter → Agency).

FAQ

How do I get paid?

Each package or task shows a fixed payout before you take it. After HQ approves your proof, the amount is credited to your Field Project Manager wallet. You can request a payout anytime (no minimum). Finance processes requests within 5 working days.

Is this the same as Restaurant or Local Sales Partner?

No. Those roles earn ongoing commission. Field Project Managers earn a fixed payout per approved package or task and do not receive residual commissions. You cannot hold Field Project Manager and a sales partner track at the same time. Strong Field Project Managers can later be upgraded by Admin.

How long do I have to finish?

48 hours when you take the work, plus one optional +24 hour extension. If the hold expires, it returns to the public list.

Do I register the business myself?

You visit, pitch, and submit proof. HQ completes the actual registration after reviewing your submission.

Is there a fixed salary?

No. Income comes only from approved packages and tasks you complete.

Which countries can I work from?

Worldwide. You take work for businesses you can physically visit. Partner HQ tools work online.
