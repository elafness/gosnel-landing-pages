About the role

This path is for people who want to do field sales (register restaurants) and also recruit other Sales Partners. You use the same GoSnel partner login — one account, two growth engines.

You must complete Sales Partner Training first. That is how you learn the wallet, Get leads playbook, and Rates & wallet story — so you can explain GoSnel to restaurant owners and to new partners you invite.

Two ways you grow

1. Field sales

  • Visit restaurants near you
  • Check → register → menu → QR → follow up
  • Earn a tier % of GoSnel revenue for 12 months per restaurant

2. Recruit Sales Partners

  • Share your affiliate invite link
  • Help them finish Training
  • Earn a one-time bonus when they reach Gold (16+ active restaurants)

What you will do

  • Complete free Training (modules + quizzes) so you can pitch with confidence.
  • Visit restaurants, use Check restaurant, explain the prepaid wallet, leave Rates & wallet PDFs.
  • Register venues, extract menus, deliver QRs, and follow up until they are live.
  • Invite friends via My Affiliates — share kit + WhatsApp templates included.
  • Coach your recruits: continue Training, use Help/FAQ, don’t overpromise hotels until Platinum.

What you get

Restaurant commissions

Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers on GoSnel revenue from restaurants you onboard (12 months each).

Affiliate bonus

One-time $100 USD when someone you invited reaches Gold (16+ active restaurants).

Free Training + tools

Get leads playbook, Help/FAQ, Rates & wallet PDF, Practice simulator, dashboard.

Same login

No second portal — Field Sales and recruiting live in one Sales Partner account.

FAQ

Is this a different login from Field Sales Partner?

No. Same Sales Partner account. Recruiting lives in My Affiliates after you apply and train.

Why do I need Training if I only want to recruit?

Potential SPs will ask about the wallet, fees, QR, and Get leads. Training (and Help) give you the same answers HQ expects you to share — fewer confused recruits and fewer support tickets.

When do I get the affiliate bonus?

When someone who applied with your link reaches Gold (16+ active restaurants). It is a one-time bonus, paid through My Referrals — not for signup alone.
