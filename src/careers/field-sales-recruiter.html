About the role

This path is for people who want to recruit Sales Partners — not visit restaurants every day. You use the same GoSnel partner login as Field Sales Partners. Your day-to-day focus is My Affiliates: invite, coach, and grow a team.

You still complete Sales Partner Training first. Recruits will ask about the wallet, fees, QR, and Get leads — Training (and Help) give you the answers HQ expects you to share.

What you will do

  • Complete free Training (modules + quizzes) so you can explain GoSnel with confidence.
  • Share your affiliate invite link from My Affiliates — share kit + WhatsApp templates included.
  • Help new partners finish Training and stay active until they reach Gold.
  • Coach recruits using Help/FAQ — don’t overpromise hotels until Platinum.
  • Earn a one-time Gold affiliate bonus when someone you invited hits 16+ active restaurants.

FAQ

Is this a different login from Field Sales Partner?

No. Same Sales Partner account. Your track focuses on recruiting in My Affiliates. Field restaurant tools can stay hidden or secondary for recruiter-track partners.

Why do I need Training if I only recruit?

Potential SPs will ask about the wallet, fees, QR, and Get leads. Training (and Help) give you the same answers HQ expects — fewer confused recruits and fewer support tickets.

When do I get the affiliate bonus?

When someone who applied with your link reaches Gold (16+ active restaurants). It is a one-time bonus, paid through My Referrals — not for signup alone.

Can I also register restaurants later?

Yes — same account. If you want field tools unlocked, ask Support / HQ to switch or expand your track. Prefer field sales from day one? Apply via the Field Sales Partner page.

How do I apply?

Click Apply now → admin.gosnel.com/apply. Process: register → choose recruiter track → Training → interview → recruit.
