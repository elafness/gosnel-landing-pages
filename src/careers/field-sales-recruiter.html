What is this role?

GoSnel needs Field Sales Partners — people who visit restaurants, register them, and earn when customers order. Your role as a Remote Recruiter Partner is to find those people and invite them to join.

You are not selling to restaurants yourself. You are building a team: post, message, invite, explain, and support — from home, a café, or anywhere with internet.

How you recruit Field Sales Partners

After free Training you get a personal invite link. Then you bring people in through channels you already use:

Social media

Post on WhatsApp Status, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Telegram, TikTok, or Snapchat. Short posts work: “Flexible commission role — visit restaurants near you, free training. Apply with my link.”

Friends & family

Message people you trust — siblings, cousins, classmates, neighbours. A personal “I thought of you for this” converts better than a public blast.

Your network

Drivers, retail staff, freelancers, university groups, community chats — anyone who is good with people and wants flexible income.

Job boards & groups

Post on remote job sites, Facebook job groups, LinkedIn, or local community boards. Always include your invite link and be clear: commission-based, free training, no fixed salary.

A day in your life (example)

There is no fixed schedule. This is just what a productive day can look like:

  1. AM

    Morning — quick posts

    Share a Status or Story with your invite link. Reply to anyone who asks “what is this?”

  2. MD

    Midday — personal messages

    WhatsApp 5–10 people you know. Explain Field Sales Partner in plain words. Send the link. Follow up politely later if they are interested.

  3. PM

    Evening — coach your invites

    Check who applied. Remind them to finish Training. Answer questions. Celebrate when someone registers their first restaurants.

Work anytime. Some people recruit for 30 minutes after dinner. Others do more on weekends. You choose when — morning, night, or between other work.

FAQ

Do I need to visit restaurants?

No. Field Sales Partners visit restaurants. You recruit and coach those Field Sales Partners — online and on your phone.

Can I really work anytime?

Yes. There are no fixed office hours. Post in the morning, message friends at night, or recruit on weekends — whatever fits your life. Consistency beats long hours.

Where should I find people?

Start with friends, family, and people you already know. Then post on social media and job groups. After Training you also get a step-by-step “How to recruit” playbook inside the platform.

Why do I need Training if I only recruit?

People you invite will ask how GoSnel works. Training gives you clear answers — so they join with confidence and stay longer.

How do I get paid?

When Field Sales Partners who applied with your link register restaurants and customers order, you earn a residual: 15% of GoSnel’s profit after their share (Silver / Gold / Platinum), for the same 12 months as their restaurant commissions. Track it on My Referrals after you join.

Is this different from Field Sales Partner?

Yes. Field Sales Partners visit restaurants. Remote Recruiter Partners invite and coach Field Sales Partners. Prefer restaurant visits? See the Field Sales Partner page.
