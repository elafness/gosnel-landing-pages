About the role

This role is for people who want to recruit Field Sales Partners — not visit restaurants every day. You invite people, help them get started, and grow a team that brings restaurants onto GoSnel.

You complete free Training first so you can explain GoSnel clearly — how it works for restaurants, how customers order, and what Field Sales Partners do day to day. That way the people you invite feel confident joining.

What you will do

  • Complete free Training so you can explain GoSnel with confidence.
  • Share your personal invite link (ready-made messages and share kit included).
  • Help new Field Sales Partners finish Training and stay active.
  • Answer common questions and coach your invites so they succeed.
  • Earn a one-time Gold bonus when someone you invited reaches 16+ active restaurants.

What you get

FAQ

Why do I need Training if I only recruit?

People you invite will ask how GoSnel works for restaurants and customers. Training gives you clear answers — so they join with confidence and stay longer.

When do I get the Gold bonus?

When someone who applied with your link reaches Gold (16+ active restaurants). It is a one-time $100 USD bonus — not for signup alone.

Is this different from Field Sales Partner?

Yes, the work is different. Field Sales Partners visit restaurants and register them. Remote Recruiter Partners invite and coach Field Sales Partners. Prefer restaurant visits? See the Field Sales Partner page.
