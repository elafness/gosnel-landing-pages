About the role

As a Local Sales Partner you introduce GoSnel to local shops and services in your city. You meet owners, help them get set up with a product catalog, and support the first steps so customers can discover and order from them.

This is not a fixed-salary office job. You earn commission on customer orders from the local partners you register, and you manage your work from a partner dashboard.

How it works

Three simple steps — easy to understand in any language.

What you will do

  • Visit flower shops, gift shops, car rentals, spas, laundry and similar businesses near you and introduce GoSnel.
  • Register them in the partner tools and help add their first products.
  • Share their login so they can manage products, prices, and availability.
  • Earn commission when customers order from the partners you registered.
  • Track partners, pipeline, and commissions in your Local Sales Partner dashboard.
  • Maintain the relationship: answer questions and help keep their catalog up to date.

FAQ

How does recurring income work?

This job is built for ongoing income, not a one-time signup fee.

When you register a local partner and it stays active on GoSnel, you can earn commission for 12 months from that partner.

During those 12 months, every time a customer orders through GoSnel from that partner, that order can add to your commission.

So your income grows when you bring more local partners and when those partners keep receiving orders.

How do I get paid?

All your earnings show in your Local Sales Partner dashboard (My Referrals / wallet area in HQ).

You can follow partner activity and commission totals there, then request a payout.

Payouts use the bank details you submit when you apply, in your local currency where supported.

How is my commission calculated?

GoSnel charges the partner a small fee on each order.

Your Local Sales Partner share is a flat 30% of GoSnel’s fee on orders from partners you registered, for up to 12 months while they stay active. There are no Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers.

In plain words: GoSnel earns a platform fee on the order, then you earn 30% of that fee.

Do I only need to register the partner?

No. Creating the account is only step one.

After registration you stay the partner’s contact. That includes:

  • Answering owner questions about products, prices, and orders
  • Helping when something is unclear or stuck
  • Helping them keep their product list complete and up to date
  • Checking in so they stay active and ready for customers

Partners that stay supported usually stay active longer, which protects your 12-month commission window.

What if the partner cancels?

If the partner cancels or leaves GoSnel, commission on that partner stops.

You only earn while the partner is active and receiving orders on the platform. That is why follow-up matters.

Which countries can I work from?

Worldwide. You can apply from any country.

You visit local shops in your own city or area, then use the online HQ tools for registration, training, pipeline, and payouts. Earnings are shown in your local currency where supported.

What do I need to start?

Basics:

  • Internet connection
  • Smartphone
  • Laptop or tablet (for registration and follow-up)

Skills that help: fluent English, good negotiation, and confidence visiting shops. Speaking the local language is a strong plus. We also give free training before you start real visits.

Do I need to pay any expenses?

No large upfront fee to join. You may spend a little on transport or simple printed materials when you visit shops — similar to other field sales roles. GoSnel provides the partner tools and training.

Is there a fixed salary?

No fixed salary. This is a commission-based Local Sales Partner role.

Your income depends on local partners you register and keep active, and on the orders those partners receive through GoSnel.

Is this a remote job?

Yes. It is remote-friendly with local field visits.

You choose when to visit shops near you. Registration, training, pipeline, and commission tracking all happen online in your Local Sales Partner dashboard.

Do I need sales experience?

Sales experience helps, but it is not required.

What matters more is clear communication, negotiation, and follow-up. We give free training and a Close a Deal playbook so you can start with a clear process.
