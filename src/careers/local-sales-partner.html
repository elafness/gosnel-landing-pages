What you sell

Hotels using GoSnel Revenue Booster show guests nearby restaurants and local services. Your job is the local partners layer: flower shops, gift shops, car rentals, coffee shops, perfume shops, spas, laundry, pharmacies, tours, and similar businesses within delivery radius of hotels.

  • Partners get a free login at partner.gosnel.com to manage their product catalog
  • Guests see partners when they are within the partner’s delivery radius of the hotel

What you will do

  • 1.Find local businesses near hotels (flowers, gifts, car rental, spa, laundry, etc.).
  • 2.Check Partner HQ that they are not already registered.
  • 3.Register them with type, pin, and delivery radius; share login once.
  • 4.Help them add products (or do a first catalog with them).
  • 5.Earn when guests order and GoSnel collects its fee — tracked in your dashboard.

How you earn

You earn a flat 30% of GoSnel’s fee for 12 months on local partners you register. Same rate as Restaurant Sales Partners. No Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers.

Example: Guest order generates AED 20 GoSnel fee. LSP gets AED 6 (30%); GoSnel keeps AED 14.

Requirements

  • Comfortable visiting local shops and speaking with owners
  • Specialist role — restaurants and hotels are separate tracks (RSP / HSP)
  • Smartphone + WhatsApp; OK with commission-based income
  • Fluent spoken English; local language is a plus

FAQ

Is this the same as Restaurant Sales Partner?

Same Partner HQ login, different track. LSP registers local partners only (shops & services). Restaurant Sales Partner (RSP) registers restaurants. Hotel Sales Partner (HSP) sells to hotels. Each is a specialist — you do not cross into the other tracks’ tools.

Do local partners pay a monthly fee?

Typically no fixed monthly fee to appear for hotel guests. GoSnel earns when guests buy; you take a flat 30% of that GoSnel fee for 12 months on partners you registered.

Where do partners manage products?

At partner.gosnel.com — Products tab. You can also add products from your LSP dashboard after registration.

Ready to apply?

Same Partner HQ as other tracks — choose Local Sales Partner on the apply form.
