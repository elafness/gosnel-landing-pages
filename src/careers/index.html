Remote Full-time

We're looking for a dynamic Sales Coordinator to join our growing team. You'll be supporting our sales efforts and helping us build strong relationships with our partners and customers.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Support the sales team in customer outreach and relationship management
  • Prepare sales reports and track customer interactions
  • Coordinate with internal teams to ensure customer satisfaction
  • Assist in developing sales strategies and campaigns
  • Maintain accurate customer database and CRM records

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field
  • 1-2 years of sales support experience
