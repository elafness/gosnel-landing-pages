About the role

As a Field Sales Partner you introduce GoSnel to restaurants in your city. You meet owners, help them get set up, and support the first steps so guests can order from QR codes, menu links, and the GoSnel app.

This is not a fixed-salary office job. You earn commission on customer meal orders from the restaurants you register, and you manage your work from a partner dashboard.

FAQ

How does recurring income work?

This job is built for ongoing income, not a one-time signup fee.

When you register a restaurant and it stays active on GoSnel, you can earn commission for 12 months from that restaurant.

During those 12 months, every time a customer orders a meal through GoSnel from that restaurant (table QR, main menu QR, social link, app, WhatsApp / Maps, and other GoSnel channels), that order can add to your commission.

So your income grows when you bring more restaurants and when those restaurants keep receiving orders.

How do I get paid?

All your earnings show in your Field Sales Partner dashboard (My Referrals / wallet area in HQ).

You can follow restaurant activity and commission totals there, then request a payout.

Payouts use the bank details you submit when you apply, in your local currency where supported.

How is my commission calculated?

GoSnel charges the restaurant a small fee on each order. The fee depends on how the guest ordered:

  • About 1.5% for Main Menu QR and Table QRs (in-restaurant ordering)
  • About 0.5% for social media menu links (Instagram / WhatsApp bio, etc.)
  • About 15% for WhatsApp / Maps delivery and pickup leads

Your Field Sales Partner share is 25% of GoSnel’s fee on orders from restaurants you registered, for up to 12 months while the restaurant stays active.

In plain words: GoSnel earns a platform fee on the order, then you earn a share of that fee.

Do I only need to register the restaurant?

No. Creating the account is only step one.

After registration you stay the restaurant’s partner contact. That includes:

  • Answering owner questions about menu, QRs, and orders
  • Helping when something is unclear or stuck
  • Reminding them to keep wallet balance topped up
  • Encouraging auto top-up so orders do not stop

Restaurants that stay supported usually stay active longer, which protects your 12-month commission window.

What if the restaurant cancels its contract?

If the restaurant cancels or leaves GoSnel, commission on that restaurant stops.

You only earn while the restaurant is active and receiving orders on the platform. That is why follow-up and auto top-up matter.

You visit restaurants in your own city or area, then use the online HQ tools for registration, training, pipeline, and payouts. Earnings are shown in your local currency where supported.

Optional, to effectively persuade restaurants to partner with you, a small investment is necessary to keep your registered restaurant visible. Essential expenses like printing QR materials are your responsibility, similar to field sales roles.

This approach is cost-effective, allowing QR codes to generate orders and commissions even after you leave.

While relying solely on digital QR codes is possible, restaurant owners may overlook them. If committed, using acrylic QR stands enhances presentation. A basic starter kit includes:

1 stand for the complete menu

3 stands for individual tables

Is there a fixed salary?

No fixed salary. This is a commission-based Field Sales Partner role.

Your income depends on restaurants you register and keep active, and on the meal orders those restaurants receive through GoSnel.

Is there an affiliate program?

Yes. After you become a Field Sales Partner, you get a referral link to invite other partners.

When someone you invite joins and grows to the Gold level, you can earn a one-time affiliate bonus. Exact bonus details are shown inside your HQ (My Affiliates) after approval.

Is this a remote job?

Yes. It is remote-friendly with local field visits.

You choose when to visit restaurants near you. Registration, training, pipeline, and commission tracking all happen online in your Field Sales Partner dashboard.

Do I need sales experience?

Sales experience helps, but it is not required.

What matters more is clear communication, negotiation, and follow-up. We give free training and a Close a Deal playbook so you can start with a clear process.
