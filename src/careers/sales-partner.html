A day in the role

Simple loop. Real money.

Find a restaurant → get the menu → register on GoSnel → deliver QRs → follow up. Track every deal in your pipeline and watch referrals grow.

  1. 1

    Find restaurants near you

    Use Maps, walk your area, or reopen warm leads. Pick places you can visit today.

  2. 2

    Meet the owner & get the menu

    Explain GoSnel simply. Collect menu PDF/photos and logo. Keep it friendly — free trial balance helps close.

  3. 3

    Register in Add restaurant

    Create their account in HQ, build the digital menu, download QR pack, and mark your pipeline Registered → QRs delivered → Contracted.

  4. 4

    Track earnings & climb tiers

    Follow up for first orders and top-ups. See commissions in My Referrals. More active restaurants = higher tier.
