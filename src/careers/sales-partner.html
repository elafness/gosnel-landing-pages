FAQ

Is this a remote job?

Yes. You work from your city. Visit local restaurants when you want, then manage registration and earnings online in GoSnel HQ.

How do I get paid?

Track everything in My Referrals and request payouts from HQ.

Do I need sales experience?

Helpful but not required. Strong negotiation and clear English matter most. We give free training and a simple Close a Deal checklist.

Is there a fixed salary?

No. This role is commission-based only. Your monthly income grows with the restaurants you bring and keep active.

What skills do you need?

Good negotiation, English language, clear communication, self-motivation, and the ability to visit restaurants nearby with a smartphone and laptop or tablet.
