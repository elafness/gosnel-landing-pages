Hotel Sales Partner (HSP)

Sell GoSnel Revenue Booster — the in-room non-room revenue engine — to hotels. Link nearby restaurants, car rentals, gift shops and more. Earn a flat 30% of GoSnel’s hotel profit/fees for 12 months.

The offer you sell

GoSnel Revenue Booster helps hotels earn on every local food and service order from the room, monetize guest screens with ads, push 5-star feedback to Google, and give guests QR access in 37+ languages — no app required.

  • Partner restaurants + marketplace addons are free to enable — GoSnel earns 10% only when guests buy

What you will do

  • 1.Visit hotels and pitch Revenue Booster (plans A / B).
  • 2.Register the hotel in Partner HQ and submit for HQ activation.
  • 3.Propose nearby restaurants, car rentals, gift shops (hotel staff must Approve each link).
  • 4.Help activate QR rooms and partner menus so guests can order from the room.
  • 5.Earn when GoSnel collects PRPM / plan fees and 10% success fees — tracked in your dashboard.

How you earn

You earn a flat 30% of GoSnel’s hotel profit/fees for 12 months on hotels you register. No Silver / Gold / Platinum tiers.

Example: Hotel pays GoSnel AED 15 PRPM. HSP gets AED 4.50 (30%); GoSnel keeps AED 10.50.
On a guest dining order, GoSnel’s success fee works the same way — you take a flat 30% of that fee for 12 months.

Requirements

  • Comfortable visiting hotels and speaking with F&B / GM / revenue managers
  • Can also register nearby restaurants and lifestyle partners
  • Smartphone + WhatsApp; OK with commission-based income
  • Fluent spoken English; local language is a plus
  • Comfortable working in Canva (menus, flyers, simple graphics)

FAQ

Is this the same as Field Sales Partner?

Same Partner HQ login, different track. HSP focuses on hotels + nearby B2B for in-room ordering and earns a flat 30% of GoSnel hotel profit/fees for 12 months. Field Sales focuses on city restaurants (App / Maps / QR) and earns a flat 30% of GoSnel’s restaurant fee for 12 months.

Do hotels pay a fixed fee for Partner Restaurants / Marketplace?

No monthly PRPM for those addons — free to enable. GoSnel takes 10% when guests order or ads sell. The hotel keeps its own 3–5% on dining. Base room plan / white-label subscription is separate.

Who approves restaurants around the hotel?

You propose partners in HQ. Hotel staff must Approve each one before guests see them (max 5 restaurants; other categories capped).
