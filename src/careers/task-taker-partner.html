About the role

Complete the task and get paid

Every task shows the payout before you take it. Finish the visit, upload proof, and when HQ approves — that amount is credited to your Task Taker wallet. Then request payout anytime (no minimum). Simple: complete → approve → paid.

This is a part-time Task Taker Partner role. You work from a public task board in Partner HQ. Each task names a business (restaurant, local shop, or hotel), a brief, and a fixed payout amount set by Admin.

You take one task at a time, visit the location, present GoSnel, collect decision-maker details and proof, then submit for HQ review. When approved, the payout is credited to your Task Taker wallet — separate from commission sales roles.

How it works

Three simple steps — easy to understand in any language.

What you will do

  • Browse open tasks and take one that fits your area (only one hold at a time).
  • Visit the restaurant, local shop, or hotel on the brief and meet the decision-maker.
  • Present GoSnel clearly and collect contact details plus any agreed commercial notes.
  • Upload proof in Partner HQ and mark the task complete for Admin review.
  • After approval, request a payout from your Task Taker wallet (no minimum).
  • Track Available → In progress → Under review → Completed in your Pipeline board.

FAQ

How do I get paid?

Each task shows a fixed payout before you take it.

After HQ approves your proof, that amount is credited to your Task Taker wallet. You can request a payout anytime (no minimum). Finance handles requests within 5 working days.

Is this the same as Restaurant or Local Sales Partner?

No. Sales partners earn ongoing commission from accounts they register. Task Takers earn a fixed payout per approved task and do not manage residual commissions.

You cannot hold Task Taker and a sales partner track at the same time.

How long do I have to finish a task?

You get 48 hours when you take a task, plus one optional +24 hour extension.

If the hold expires, the task returns to the public list for someone else.

Do I register the business myself?

You visit, pitch, and submit proof. HQ completes the real vendor or hotel registration after reviewing your submission.

Is there a fixed salary?

No fixed salary. Income comes from approved tasks you complete.

Which countries can I work from?

Worldwide. You take tasks for businesses you can visit in your area. Partner HQ tools work online.
