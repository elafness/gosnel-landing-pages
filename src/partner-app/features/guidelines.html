Partner guidelines

Clear standards for local shops that serve residents in nearby towers: grocery, pharmacy, meals, water, laundry, and more.

Service quality

  • Accurate fulfilment. Deliver what the resident ordered. Confirm substitutions before you pack when something is unavailable.
  • Fresh and safe goods. Follow UAE health and municipal rules for your category (food, pharmacy, water, and so on).
  • Clear packaging. Label bags or boxes so building staff and residents can identify the order quickly.
  • Honest catalogue. Keep prices, photos, and item names up to date in GoSnel.

Orders and response times

  • Accept promptly. Review new requests as soon as you can and accept or decline with a clear reason.
  • Realistic ETAs. Set preparation and delivery times you can keep during peak hours.
  • Status updates. Mark packing, on the way, and delivered so residents see progress in the app.
  • Building access. Follow tower rules for parking, concierge, and door delivery.

Category-specific rules (for example pharmacy dispensing or food safety) still apply on top of these shared GoSnel standards.

Licensing and compliance

  • Valid trade licence. Keep your UAE business licence current for the activities you offer.
  • Category permits. Hold any required health, municipality, or professional permits for your service.
  • Insurance. Carry liability cover appropriate for deliveries to residential buildings.
  • Data and privacy. Use resident details only to fulfil orders. Do not share contact data outside GoSnel fulfilment.

Resident experience

  • Professional communication. Keep messages clear and polite in the order channel.
  • Issue handling. Fix missing or wrong items quickly, or work with GoSnel support when needed.
  • Ratings. Strong service helps you stay visible to nearby buildings on GoSnel.
