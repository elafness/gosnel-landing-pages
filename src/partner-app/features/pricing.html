Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

What does "Lifetime" mean?

Lifetime means you pay once and enjoy the package forever. No monthly fees, no annual renewals. One payment, lifetime access.

Is the Free package really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No credit card required. You get 50 AED operating credit and can list up to 3 combo sets. Perfect to test the platform.

What is the 15% commission for?

The 15% commission is charged per order and covers the platform usage, marketing, and customer acquisition. This is significantly lower than other platforms that charge 30%+.

What is Operating Credit?

Operating Credit is promotional balance you can use to boost your restaurant visibility, run campaigns, and attract more customers on the platform.

What is WhatsApp Deep-link Ordering?

Customers browse your menu, select items, and the order is sent directly to your WhatsApp. Simple, no complicated apps to manage.

Can I upgrade from Free to Golden or Diamond?

Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime. Just contact us on WhatsApp and we'll help you transition smoothly. Your existing data and customers will be preserved.

What is Multi-Branch Global Sync?

Diamond package allows you to manage all your restaurant branches from one dashboard. Update menus, pricing, and promotions across all locations simultaneously.

What is a Prepaid Wallet?

Your prepaid wallet is a balance account we use to manage commissions. Since you collect cash directly from customers, we deduct the 15% commission from this wallet balance automatically. You start with FREE credit (50 AED for Free/Golden, 250 AED for Diamond).

How Does the Wallet Commission Work?

Here's the simple flow:

  • Customer places order for 100 AED
  • You collect 100 AED in cash from the customer
  • We automatically deduct 15 AED (15%) from your wallet balance
  • You keep 85 AED in your pocket instantly

What Happens When My Wallet Runs Out?

When your wallet balance gets low, you'll receive a notification. You can top up your wallet anytime with any amount you choose. This keeps your restaurant active and visible in the app. Most restaurants top up in small amounts as needed.

Can I See My Wallet Balance Anytime?

Yes! You can track your wallet balance in real-time through your restaurant dashboard. You'll see:

  • Current wallet balance
  • All transactions (commissions deducted)
  • Top-up history
  • Commission breakdown per order

How is This Different From Other Apps?

Why our system is better:

  • You keep 85% instantly - not waiting for settlement (other apps make you wait 7-14 days)
  • 15% commission only - not 25-30% like other platforms
  • No hidden fees - commission is transparent and automatic
  • You control when to top up - not forced monthly/annual payments
  • Free starting credit - no upfront investment required
